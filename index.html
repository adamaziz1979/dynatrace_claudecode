Executive Summary
Dynatrace has grown revenue from US$546m (FY20) to US$2,018m (FY26) by selling an AI-powered observability platform to the world's largest companies. Since 2023 customers buy through consumption-based subscriptions, so Dynatrace only grows when customers use more of the platform. Growth is now slowing: FY27 ARR guidance of US$2.38bn to US$2.40bn sent the shares down 11.4% on 13 May 2026 amid doubts about pricing power. APAC is a US$193m region growing 19%. The Business Insights manager's job is to turn platform data into measurable customer value that grows consumption and secures renewals in APAC's top accounts.

What is Grail?

Grail is Dynatrace's own data store, and the data the Business Insights team works with lives there.

What it is

  • Observed Fact Launched on 4 Oct 2022 as a "causational data lakehouse" with a massively parallel processing (MPP) analytics engine and the new Dynatrace Query Language (DQL). Its first use was log analytics, initially on AWS.
  • Observed Fact Built on an indexless, schema-on-read design: structure is applied when the data is read, so nobody designs tables or re-indexes before data arrives.
  • Observed Fact The FY2026 10-K calls it Dynatrace's "unified data foundation", holding logs, traces, metrics and user sessions "without the need for upfront indexing, schema design, or data movement".
  • Observed Fact Dynatrace now markets it as "the unified data lakehouse built for AI-driven operations", with always-on hot and cold storage and OpenPipeline ingestion.

What sits in it

  • Observed Fact Logs, metrics, traces, real-user (RUM) sessions, security data and business events.
  • Observed Fact Business events (from Nov 2022) capture commercial signals such as order fulfilment, bill payments, service activation and customer onboarding. Sources include OneAgent, web and mobile RUM sessions and a public API.
  • Observed Fact Data is mapped to Smartscape, Dynatrace's live topology of how services, hosts and applications connect. Strong Inference This is what lets a query link a revenue drop to the service causing it.

How it's used

  • Observed Fact Analysts query it with DQL, which parses, transforms and analyses data at read time. Results feed Notebooks, Dashboards and platform apps.
  • Observed Fact The Business Insights app links its value moments into Notebooks, Dashboards and other platform apps.
  • Strong Inference Dynatrace Intelligence's AI agents (Jan 2026) reason over the same unified data.
  • Strong Inference Under DPS, ingesting, retaining and querying Grail data is consumption. More analysis means more revenue.
Why it matters for this role
  • H1 in practice: every dashboard, query and business-event use case the squad builds runs on Grail. Proving value and growing consumption are the same activity.
  • Your Cisco parallels: your Snowflake and dbt work gives you the vocabulary (lineage, governed datasets, release cycles). Your OpenTelemetry endpoint project gives you the experience: collection intervals, metric volume and privacy controls are the trade-offs Grail customers face.
  • Working Hypothesis Likely gap: you won't have used DQL. Spend an hour in the free trial or the DQL docs before the practical, so you can say you've written queries rather than just read about them.
Questions worth asking the panel "How much of the Business Insights work now runs on Grail and business events, compared with classic digital experience dashboards?"
"Do analysts build in DQL and Notebooks themselves, or does a platform team do that?"

Business Insights App

Observed Fact The app moves Dynatrace's Business Insights service inside the platform, replacing email and calls. Your squad's analysts would publish their work here, and customers would read, comment on and rate it.

The key term: "value moment"

Observed Fact A value moment is a short, consumable analysis of digital experience data that an analyst publishes. Each one is tagged by value chain (Operational, Performance, Business or Other), component (Synthetic, Web app, Mobile app) and target (application or monitor, page or event).

Main features

  • Observed Fact Timeline: one history of value moments, announcements, enablement material and invitations to Tech Talks and Customer Connects. Dynatrace says it also helps new customer staff get up to speed.
  • Observed Fact Value moment detail: optional video summary, indexed sections, links into Notebooks, Dashboards and other apps, and a shareable deep link.
  • Observed Fact Comments: customers highlight any content to ask a question; the Insights team is notified and replies in the thread, with email alerts.
  • Observed Fact Feedback: a satisfied or dissatisfied rating with optional context on every value moment, used to steer future analysis.
  • Observed Fact Search, Favourites and a Contact Insights Team tab.

Access

Observed Fact The app is not self-serve. It comes with the Business Insights service: the customer gets a token from the Insights team, subscribes to the Business Insights channel and installs the app from Dynatrace Hub. Users need the insights:moments:read permission. It is available on Dynatrace SaaS.
